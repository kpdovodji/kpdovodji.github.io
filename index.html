Pierre Dovodji

Kuassi Pierre Dovodji is a PhD student in applied mathematics (statistics) at the Institut de Mathématiques de Bourgogne (IMB, UMR CNRS 5584), Université Bourgogne Europe, in Dijon, France. His PhD is supervised by Hervé Cardot (IMB) and David Nerini (Mediterranean Institute of Oceanography, Aix-Marseille University).

He develops statistical methods to reconstruct ocean fields from noisy and irregularly sampled satellite measurements. His work uses space-time radial basis function (RBF) splines and functional data analysis, with applications to sea surface height in the Gulf Stream and to surface chlorophyll in the Southern Ocean.
