Research of Pierre Dovodji

PhD thesis: New statistical models for functional data motivated by applications in oceanography, Université Bourgogne Europe, supervised by Hervé Cardot and David Nerini.

Reconstruction of spatio-temporal fields from noisy and irregularly sampled satellite data with space-time radial basis function (RBF) splines. Applications to sea surface height in the Gulf Stream and to surface chlorophyll in the Southern Ocean.
